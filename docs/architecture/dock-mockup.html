How the Dock is built

Rules the mockup follows

  • Requested and actual size. tilesize goes from 16 to 128 px, but the Dock never crosses the screen edge. The actual size is the smaller of the request and what fits: min(tilesize, usable width ÷ (icons + 0.36 × separators)). More apps lower the maximum.
  • Pitch = size. Each icon takes a square slot of tilesize; the artwork keeps its own margin (an 824 body on a 1024 grid), so there is no gap between icons.
  • Magnification. Each icon grows with its distance to the pointer along a cosine curve with a radius of 3 icons, up to largesize. It grows away from the bar; the bar only gets wider.
  • Dots. Centered in the padding between the icon and the screen edge, not inside the icon. The diameter follows the size (2 to 5 px).
  • Bounce on launch. The icon jumps half its height and falls while the app starts; without launchanim, only the dot appears.
  • Minimize. Genie, scale or suck into the window slot right of the separator, or into the app icon with minimize-to-application.
  • Remove and reorder. Dragging inside reorders; dragging out shows “Remove” and drops a puff. An open app that is removed stays in the Dock until it quits.
  • Name bubble over the icon under the pointer, with an arrow toward it.
